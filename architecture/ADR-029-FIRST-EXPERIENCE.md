# ADR-029 — Purpose Before Mechanics

**Status:** Accepted  
**Release:** v0.66.0

## Context
The existing onboarding described body needs, six dimensions and the Living Model before a new person had a simple mental model of the product. The architecture was accurate, but the value proposition remained implicit.

## Decision
The first experience must answer, in order:
1. Why the product exists.
2. What Strategos does.
3. What the daily experience feels like.
4. What minimal information is needed to begin.

Architecture names, Advisors, scores and feature tours remain absent. The product promise is expressed as understanding today before acting, one direction at a time, with the person retaining the decision.

## Consequences
- New users reach the first daily check-in with a correct mental model.
- The premium visual baseline is preserved.
- Existing users are not forced through onboarding again; it remains available through replay in Settings.
