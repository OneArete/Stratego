# ADR-009 — Current Moment Foundation

**Status:** Accepted and frozen  
**Release:** v0.44.0

## Decision

Strategos resolves the person's current moment before choosing what to render. Pages remain implementation details; the visible experience presents one next action.

The canonical progression is:

`Arrive → Understand → Deliberate → Act → Reflect → Continue`

These state names are never exposed to the person.

## Consequences

- A partial check-in resumes at the first unanswered signal.
- The daily check-in presents one question at a time.
- “Why” is absent until a real current-day recommendation exists.
- A closed day exposes no call to action.
- The Human Model cannot substitute current-day evidence.
