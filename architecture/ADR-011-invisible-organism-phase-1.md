# ADR-011 — Invisible Organism, Phase 1

Status: Accepted and frozen

## Decision

The primary Today experience is no longer a dashboard. It is a Current Moment composed of exactly three visible layers:

1. the Living Human Organism;
2. one sentence describing the present moment;
3. at most one next action.

Internal architecture, evidence stages, Agora activity, scores and technical state remain invisible.

The daily check-in is presented as a continuous encounter with the organism. Six context seeds become alive only after explicit answers. Silence never creates evidence.

## Constraints

- One primary action on Today.
- No recommendation before the Evidence Gate is satisfied.
- No `Why` before a real current-day recommendation exists.
- A closed day has no call to action.
- The Human Model never substitutes current-day evidence.
- Visual presence precedes explanatory text.
- Existing constitutional navigation destinations remain available while the experience layer is progressively replaced.

## Consequences

Most dashboard styling and redundant cards are bypassed on Today and check-in. The next phases will progressively replace recommendation, Practice, Reflection, Journey and Settings without weakening the underlying engines.
