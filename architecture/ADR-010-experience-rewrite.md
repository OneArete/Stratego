# ADR-010 — Experience Rewrite

Status: Accepted and frozen

## Decision

Strategos presents the current human moment rather than exposing its internal architecture.

- Today presents one primary next action.
- The daily check-in asks one question at a time and returns automatically when sufficient explicit evidence exists.
- Deliberation remains an internal mechanism. The interface does not expose Advisor names, pipeline stages or system-state language.
- The Living Human Graph accompanies context gathering as a visual manifestation of evidence, never as a score.
- No recommendation exists before the Evidence Gate is satisfied.

## Constitutional basis

The person sees the moment and the next step. The software retains the architecture.
