# Practice Experience Governance — v0.34

The focused Practice surface is a presentation layer over the frozen Practice Engine.

## Product rule

During execution the person sees one primary cue, the timer, current phase, next phase and essential controls. Technique, regressions and progressions remain available through progressive disclosure.

## Authority boundaries

- Person control remains authoritative.
- Safety interruption remains authoritative.
- The Practice contract and canonical phase content remain unchanged.
- The surface never advances, adapts or ends a Practice automatically beyond existing engine rules.
- Presentation influence on judgement, learning and safety is zero.
