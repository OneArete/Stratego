# Evening Experience Governance — v0.35.0

## Purpose
Close each Daily Story with a calm, optional one-minute reflection.

## Constitutional boundaries
- Writing is voluntary.
- A day may be closed without writing.
- Free text is not analysed, scored, diagnosed or used to infer themes.
- Closing a day does not classify it as good, bad, successful or failed.
- Judgement, Human Model and safety influence remain 0.
- Existing Practice reflection remains the source for outcome learning.

## Product rule
The evening surface asks one question: **What mattered today?** Complexity remains available in the Emotional Journal, not imposed in the daily flow.
