# OneArete Experience Constitution

**Status:** FROZEN

1. The person sees the present moment and one next step, never the architecture.
2. Strategos never pretends to know.
3. One screen has one primary objective.
4. The organism communicates; text explains.
5. Silence is a valid state.
6. No hidden gamification, guilt or manufactured urgency.
7. The Human Model is not reduced to a score.
8. Difficulty is represented without judgement of personal worth.
9. The organism represents state, relationship, readiness and potential—not performance.
10. The organism remains neutral when evidence is absent.
11. Intelligence must become progressively less visible.
12. Trust takes years to build and one false claim to damage.
13. Simplicity is earned through depth.
14. Strategos advises; the person decides and lives.
15. The best interaction is the one the person never notices.
