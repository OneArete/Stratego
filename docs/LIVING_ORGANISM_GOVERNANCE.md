# Living Organism Governance

Normative foundation introduced in Strategos v0.43.0.

## Purpose

The Living Human Graph represents state, relationship and potential for change. It does not represent worth, rank or performance.

## Rules

1. One shared Life Clock coordinates the visible organism.
2. Motion must remain slow enough to be perceived as life rather than interface feedback.
3. Geometry may contain bounded asymmetry, but must never fabricate personal evidence.
4. Filament tension may express relationships already supported by the Human Model; it may not imply unsupported causality.
5. The Self remains visually stable. Domain availability may change; human worth does not.
6. Red/green semaphores, scores, streaks and reward animation are prohibited.
7. Reduced-motion preferences must produce a complete static representation.
8. Longitudinal scars, seasons and persistent morphology remain out of scope until supported by explicit evidence architecture.
