# Daily Story Governance

The day is the primary experiential unit of Strategos from v0.32 onward.

A Daily Story may contain explicit context, a judgement, a Practice, an outcome reflection and an optional evening reflection. It is a continuity object, not a performance score.

## Boundaries
- Missing stages remain missing; Strategos does not fabricate continuity.
- A day is never labelled good, bad, successful or failed.
- Evening reflection remains optional.
- Daily Story does not create beliefs, diagnoses or character claims.
- Existing audit records remain authoritative for their own domains.
