# Dynamics of Human Development

Status: integrated research architecture for v0.26. Not constitutional canon.

## Purpose

Dynamics of Human Development is the scientific layer that studies how a person changes over time. It supports Human Flourishing Engineering by supplying explicit, testable models of trajectory, timescale, prediction, resource use and revision.

## Core propositions

1. A person is not adequately represented by state alone. The model must distinguish state, direction of change and rate of change.
2. Human variables evolve on different timescales. Transient state must not silently rewrite slow-changing beliefs, identity or purpose.
3. Learning begins with a prediction–outcome comparison. Prediction error is evidence, not judgement.
4. Strategos does not maximise isolated variables. It supports sustainable flourishing across competing domains and constraints.
5. Every choice consumes or restores human resources, including time, energy, attention, recovery, social capital and future freedom.
6. Decisions have first-, second- and higher-order consequences. Future simulation must expose benefit, cost, risk and opportunity cost.
7. Model changes must be explicit, reviewable and reversible. No belief may change silently.

## Human development cycle

Signals → Living Human Model → Prediction → Future Simulation → Agora → Choice → Practice → Outcome → Prediction Error → Model Revision → Identity and Character Development → Flourishing.

## Temporal architecture

The Human Model must eventually support at least five clocks:

- immediate: minutes to hours;
- daily: sleep, fatigue, mood and recovery;
- adaptive: weeks;
- developmental: months;
- longitudinal: years.

Each model variable requires a declared update policy and temporal persistence. The current v0.26 implementation does not yet calculate trajectories or half-lives. It creates the trustworthy outcome substrate required before those engines can exist.

## Epistemic boundaries

- An outcome is not proof of causality.
- A single outcome does not revise the Human Model.
- Person-reported outcomes and scientific evidence remain separate.
- Unknown outcomes remain unknown.
- Counterfactuals must never be presented as observed facts.
- Identity and character must not be scored from isolated behaviour.

## v0.26 implementation sequence

1. Outcome Ledger: immutable, judgement-linked outcome records.
2. Personal Evidence: reviewed aggregation of comparable outcomes.
3. Confidence Calibration: confidence adjusted by evidence quantity, quality and similarity.
4. Strategy Memory: retrieval of genuinely comparable prior situations.
5. Learning Audit: calibration, overconfidence and underconfidence review.
6. Prediction Engine: bounded predictions with declared uncertainty.
7. Model Revision: explicit belief-change proposals reviewed before activation.
8. Future and Value Engines: multi-path simulation with costs and value trade-offs.

## Architectural rule

The Living Human Model is not allowed to learn directly from raw events. Learning requires an explicit chain from decision context to action, outcome, evidence review and model revision.
