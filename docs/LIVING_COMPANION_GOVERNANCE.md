# Living Companion Governance

## Decision

Strategos is a calm daily companion, not a dashboard and not an engagement machine.

The first surface of Today presents one living presence, one current judgement and one next action. Supporting reasoning remains available through explicit progressive disclosure.

## Frozen interaction principles

1. The person remains the protagonist.
2. The Living Human Graph is persistent, quiet and non-decorative.
3. Strategos does not manufacture urgency, novelty or notifications.
4. Silence is a valid product state.
5. When nothing material has changed, Strategos may say: “Nothing new today. Continue your story.”
6. Existing safety, Practice, voice, sound, wake-lock, agency and audit behaviour remain unchanged.
7. Hidden complexity must remain accessible and auditable.

## Behavioural influence

This experience layer changes presentation and continuity only. It does not change judgement formation, safety logic, the Human Model or learning influence.
