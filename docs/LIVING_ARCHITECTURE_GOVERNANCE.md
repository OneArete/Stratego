# Living Architecture Governance

Strategos v0.39.0 makes the Living Human Graph the primary visible expression of the Human Model.

## Permitted inputs
- current daily context;
- recorded history;
- confidence and momentum already represented by the Human Graph model.

## Prohibited claims
The visual organism must not imply diagnosis, precise measurement, character, worth, causality or certainty not supported by the underlying evidence.

## Interaction contract
- The graph may breathe and adapt slowly.
- Domain differences may alter emphasis and connection strength.
- The centre remains visually stable.
- Textual explanation remains available separately.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Product boundary
The organism is an interpretive interface, not a clinical instrument or score.
