# OneArete Visual Philosophy — Living Architecture

## Status
Normative foundation introduced in Strategos v0.39.0.

## Core distinction
Information about the person is represented as life. Information about software is represented as structure.

The Living Human Graph is therefore not an analytical radar, score display, decoration or engagement mechanism. It is the quiet, living expression of the Human Model.

## Principles

1. **Meaning before spectacle.** Motion must communicate human state, continuity, tension or adaptation. Decorative motion is excluded.
2. **The Self remains stable.** Domain availability may vary; the person's worth never rises or falls.
3. **Life is slow.** Organic movement is continuous, calm and non-urgent. No flashing, bouncing or reward animation.
4. **Silence is valid.** The system does not manufacture novelty when nothing important has changed.
5. **Progress is not gamified.** Growth appears as continuity, coherence and accumulated form, never as points or streak pressure.
6. **Tension is not alarm.** Imbalance is conveyed through relationship and form, not red warnings or moral judgement.
7. **Complexity remains beneath the surface.** Explanation is available on demand; the first perception should be intuitive.
8. **Accessibility overrides animation.** Reduced-motion preferences remove non-essential movement without removing meaning.

## Current implementation boundary
v0.39.0 establishes a genuine six-domain living organism in Today and preserves the detailed Graph in Understanding. It does not yet claim longitudinal scars, persistent morphology, domain interaction or biographical growth. Those require explicit evidence architecture and will not be simulated prematurely.
