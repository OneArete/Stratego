# Weekly Review Governance

The Weekly Review is a person-visible, descriptive snapshot of the last seven calendar days.

## Rules

- It becomes available after at least three recorded days.
- It reports only explicit records already held by Strategos.
- It does not score the person, infer character, diagnose, or claim causality.
- A review is preserved only after an explicit person action.
- Preserved reviews are immutable snapshots; later records do not silently rewrite them.
- Automatic Human Model, judgement, Practice selection, and safety influence remain zero.
