# Strategos — Practice Contract

Status: v0.19 Phase 1
Scope: pre-action clarity

A Practice must state, before execution:

```text
what it is trying to change
what dose is being attempted
what counts as enough
when to stop
what reflection will later evaluate
what remains uncertain
```

The contract is not consent to an obligation and not a promise of outcome.

At the moment the Practice begins, Strategos snapshots the contract so that later reflection can be compared with the expectation that existed before action.


## Phase 2 — Resolution

After reflection, Strategos resolves the preserved contract as:

```text
aligned
partially aligned
misaligned
uncertain
```

This resolution compares the original expected effect with effect, goal fit, burden and completion evidence. It is an accountable comparison, not a causal proof.


## Phase 3 — Calibration candidates

Repeated resolved contracts may form a calibration candidate for the same Practice after three comparable outcomes within 180 days.

The candidate may be:

```text
mostly aligned
mixed
often misaligned
```

This is visible evidence only. It does not rewrite future contracts, alter expected effects or influence judgement selection.


## Phase 4 — Person review

Calibration candidates are not accepted silently. The person may confirm them as accurate, reject them as misleading, or reopen a prior decision.

Review changes accountability status only:

```text
contract influence: 0
judgement influence: 0
```


## Phase 5 — Person-authorised revision

A confirmed calibration may generate a proposed change to the expected-effect wording.

The proposal is inert until the person accepts it.

```text
confirmed calibration
→ revision proposal
→ person accepts or declines
→ accepted wording applies only to the same Practice
```

The revision changes only the expected-effect statement. It does not alter Practice selection, duration, safety boundaries, reflection logic or judgement confidence.
