# Strategos — Emotional Journal Governance

Status: v0.25 Phase 1

## Purpose

A private, voluntary, end-of-day free-writing space.

The person may write about:

```text
what went well
what went badly
what remains emotionally present
anything else they choose
```

## Phase 1 rules

- entirely optional;
- free text;
- no mood score;
- no required prompts;
- no diagnosis;
- no automated theme extraction;
- no automatic Human Model evidence;
- no influence on judgement, safety or Practice selection;
- editable and deletable;
- stored locally with the Strategos state;
- visible in Journey as personal continuity.

## Operational boundary

```text
judgement influence: 0
Human Model influence: 0
safety influence: 0
```

The diary belongs to Reflection and Journey. It does not silently become evidence about the person.

## Phase 2 — Person-selected themes and reflective continuity

The person may optionally attach body, mind, recovery, relationships, purpose, work, gratitude or difficulty.

Themes are never inferred from text. Only explicit selections appear in Personal Evolution and Reflective Continuity.

Judgement, Human Model and safety influence remain zero.

## Phase 3 — Person-reported reflection follow-up

A person may later review an entry as still present, changed, resolved or uncertain. An optional note may explain what evolved.

The status is never inferred from text and can be reopened.

Judgement, Human Model and safety influence remain zero.
