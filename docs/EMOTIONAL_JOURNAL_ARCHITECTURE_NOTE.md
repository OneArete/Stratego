# Strategos — Emotional Journal Architecture Note

Status: accepted direction, not yet implemented

## Purpose

A voluntary end-of-day free-writing space where the person may record what went well, what went badly, what remains emotionally present, or anything else they choose.

## Principles

- optional and free text;
- no mandatory mood score;
- no diagnosis or automated psychological interpretation;
- no manipulative streaks;
- editable, exportable and deletable;
- visible in Journey as personal reflection;
- influence on judgement remains zero until a separate governed release;
- local-first and private by default.

## Placement

Reflection and Journey layer. It does not become Human Model evidence by default.
