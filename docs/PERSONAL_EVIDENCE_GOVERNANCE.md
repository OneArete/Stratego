# Personal Evidence Governance — v0.27 Phase 1

## Purpose

Personal Evidence turns repeated, person-reported outcomes into an understandable summary of what has tended to help this person. It does not infer causality and does not silently modify the Living Human Model or future judgements.

## Evidence boundary

A record is eligible only when:

- the person reported a directional outcome;
- an execution outcome is linked;
- the decision context was frozen at the time of judgement.

Unknown outcomes are preserved in the Outcome Ledger but excluded from directional summaries.

## Minimum evidence

A pattern is not displayed as personal evidence until at least three directional outcomes exist for the same Practice. The evidence level is then shown as exploratory, emerging, or established according to the number of observations.

## Weighted helpfulness

- Helped = 1.0
- Helped partly = 0.5
- Did not help = 0.0

This is a transparent descriptive summary, not a clinical score and not proof of causality.

## Constitutional limits

- Automatic Human Model influence: 0
- Automatic judgement influence: 0
- Automatic Practice selection influence: 0
- Automatic safety influence: 0
- No free-text interpretation
- No hidden confidence changes

Personal Evidence is visible to the person before it is ever allowed to participate in deliberation.
