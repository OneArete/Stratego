# Weekly Intention Governance

## Status
Implemented in Strategos v0.37.0.

A Weekly Intention is a direction explicitly selected by the person after reviewing the last seven calendar days.

## Constitutional limits
- Strategos does not select the intention.
- The intention is not a goal score, diagnosis, belief, character claim or inferred mission.
- It remains editable and removable by the person.
- Automatic Human Model influence is `0`.
- Automatic judgement influence is `0`.
- Automatic Practice selection influence is `0`.
- It may be displayed as declared context, but must never be represented as evidence of behaviour or outcome.
